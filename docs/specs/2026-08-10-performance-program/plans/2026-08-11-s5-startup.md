## Scope and design anchors

- R17 requires restore-time surface preparation not to delay usable startup,
  a measured improvement versus baseline, and interactive failure presentation
  through the existing health/recovery flow
  (`2026-08-10-performance-program.md:300-307`).
- The normative structure keeps AppKit/Ghostty objects on MainActor. The
  accepted restore cohort publishes placeholders first; a one-shot first-frame
  fact comes from `ApplicationLifecycleMonitor`, is stored in
  `WindowLifecycleAtom`, and gates serial surface creation with MainActor yields
  (`program-design.md:454-533,685-697`).
- `WorkspaceSurfaceCoordinator.registerTerminalPlaceholderIfNeeded` remains the
  placeholder owner. `TerminalActivationScheduler` retains cohort, priority,
  retry, replacement, and settlement ownership. `SurfaceManager` retains
  creation, attachment, health, and destruction ownership
  (`program-design.md:480-520`).
- Stored `TerminalState.zmxSessionID` remains authoritative. Deferral cannot
  generate a replacement identity, rewrite restored composition, or turn a
  failed exact attach into an ephemeral shell (`program-design.md:503-520`).
- R2 adds two measure-first surfaces in this slice: startup time-to-usable and
  Ghostty renderer frame begin-to-present. Renderer optimization is explicitly
  out of scope (`program-design.md:699-705`; Specification R2/C1/V2).
- R20 requires every slice-1 priority interaction, including Cmd+R, to remain
  at-or-better than its preceding completed baseline and inside the existing
  frame gates (`2026-08-10-performance-program.md:325-331`).
- R18 has no D1-D16 or M1-M12 item assigned to slice 5 in the closed inventory.
  Slice 5 still updates the architecture documents whose startup placement and
  renderer-probe claims change. R19 checks all four R4 guards against the
  cleaned write surfaces and flips only an actually applicable guard.

## False-green risks and stop/replan conditions

- Layout settled, bounds nonempty, `app.did_finish_launching`, placeholder
  registration, and first rendered interactive frame are distinct facts.
  Equating any two is a false green.
- Registering placeholders after scheduler activation does not satisfy T1 even
  if they appear before the first surface becomes ready.
- `Task.yield()` before a concurrent four-worker fleet is not serial creation;
  maximum restore-time MainActor creation admission must be one.
- Marking aggregate restore complete at placeholder publication can corrupt
  focus, repair, or registry settlement. Preserve final cohort settlement.
- Moving configuration, `NSView`, pointers, C strings, or
  `ghostty_surface_new` off-main without a proven supported API violates the
  design and protected surface.
- Health-change notification, surface-init success, view attachment, and frame
  presentation are not interchangeable renderer-present endpoints.
- Unit tests, fake ports, state files, raw Victoria queries, stale markers,
  JSONL, or `app.did_finish_launching` cannot substitute for V6/V8.
- Stop with `[BLOCKER-FOR-PARENT]` if the baseline rail remains absent after
  restack; first-frame completion cannot be owned by existing AppKit ingress;
  a single MainActor surface creation still misses V6; renderer endpoints
  require vendor internals; exact zmx identity cannot survive deferral;
  placeholder publication changes composition/focus ownership; a schema,
  vendor, trust, or public-contract change appears; or an R4 flip requires
  weakening/deleting a gate.
